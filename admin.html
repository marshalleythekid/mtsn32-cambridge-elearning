What admins can do

  • Teachers: add accounts, reset passwords, make someone an admin, disable an account when a teacher leaves.
  • Students: add one by one or import a class list, reset passwords, move to another class, disable when they leave.
  • Classes: add classes and set the homeroom teacher.
  • Lessons: hide a lesson from students without deleting it.
  • Announcements: post to every class, end any announcement.
  • Activity log: every account change is recorded with who did it.

Students and teachers who forgot a password come to the Cambridge Room or the Teachers’ Room. There is no reset email.
